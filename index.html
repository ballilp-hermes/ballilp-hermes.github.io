Hermes Personal Assistant

Private, single-user desktop automation · operated by the owner of the Google Account that authorises it

The purpose of this application is to carry out everyday administrative tasks for one person inside their own Google Account — adding events to their calendar, organising and drafting their email, and producing documents and spreadsheets from their own files — so those tasks can be done by instruction instead of by hand.

What Hermes Personal Assistant is

Hermes Personal Assistant is a desktop automation tool. It runs locally on its owner's own Mac and performs work the owner asks for directly, in plain language or as a written instruction.

It is a private tool, not a public product. There is no sign-up, no user accounts, no dashboard, no subscription and no multi-user service. It is not distributed to the public and is not published in any app store. It has exactly one user: the person who owns the Google Account that authorises it. That person is also the only person whose data the application can reach.

What it does

  • Calendar — lists the owner's events and creates new events when asked, including titles, times, locations and reminders.
  • Email — searches and labels the owner's mail, drafts replies, and sends a message only after the owner has approved that specific message.
  • Drive, Sheets and Docs — locates and reads the owner's own files, and writes new documents, reports and spreadsheet rows from them.
  • Contacts — resolves a name to an email address. Read-only; contacts are never created, edited or deleted.

Why it asks for Google permissions

Each permission maps to one of the tasks above, and nothing more. Access is requested through Google's standard OAuth 2.0 consent screen, scope by scope, and only after the owner reviews and approves the request. The exact scopes are listed, with their purpose, on the Privacy Policy page. The owner can withdraw access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which takes effect immediately.

What it never does: no advertising, no profiling, no analytics or tracking, no sale or resale of personal data, no credit, insurance or lending decisions, no use of Google user data to train generalised or third-party machine-learning models, and no access to any Google Account other than the authorising owner's.

Where the data lives

OAuth tokens and working files stay on the owner's own computer, protected by owner-only file permissions. There is no application server that stores Google user data and no third party that receives it. Requests go directly from that machine to Google's APIs, and only for the task at hand.

Compliance

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The application collects no data for advertising and does not transfer Google user data to third parties except where necessary to provide a feature the owner has requested, for security purposes, or to comply with applicable law.

Privacy

Full detail — every scope, the purpose, storage location, retention, security measures, and how to revoke access or request deletion — is set out in the Privacy Policy.
